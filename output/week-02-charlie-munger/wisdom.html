CHAPTER ONE
Invert, Always Invert
逆向思考
先找死路，再找生路
Find Where You'll Die First
正文Body

芒格从数学家雅各比那里借来一句箴言：「反过来想，总是反过来想。」与其研究如何成功，不如系统列出所有可能导致失败的路径，然后逐一规避。

Munger borrowed a line from mathematician Carl Jacobi: "Invert, always invert." Instead of studying how to succeed, systematically list every path to failure — then avoid them one by one.

他的经典表述是：「我只想知道我会死在哪里，这样我就永远不去那里。」这不是悲观，而是一种工程思维——先排除灾难，再谈优化。

His classic formulation: "All I want to know is where I'm going to die, so I'll never go there." This isn't pessimism — it's engineering: eliminate catastrophe first, optimize second.

核心洞察Core Insight

成功往往来自避开愚蠢，而非追逐聪明。

"Invert, always invert." — Carl Jacobi, via Charlie Munger

Invert, Always Invert
逆向思考
清单的力量
The Power of Checklists
正文Body

芒格和巴菲特在航空业吃过亏后，建立了一份「不投资清单」：不碰不懂的业务、不投管理层不诚信的公司、不追逐热门概念。清单不是限制，是护栏。

After losing money in airlines, Munger and Buffett built a "don't invest" checklist: no businesses they don't understand, no dishonest management, no hot trends. A checklist isn't a cage — it's a guardrail.

普通人同样可以建立「人生不做什么」清单：不嫉妒、不抱怨、不碰杠杆、远离有毒的人。芒格说，避免愚蠢比追求聪明更可靠。

Ordinary people can build a "what not to do" list too: no envy, no complaining, no leverage, no toxic people. Munger's point: avoiding stupidity is more reliable than chasing brilliance.

核心洞察Core Insight

用清单排除灾难，比用灵感追逐成功更管用。

"It is remarkable how much long-term advantage people like us have gotten by trying to be consistently not stupid, instead of trying to be very intelligent."

A Latticework of Mental Models
多元思维模型
手里只有锤子的人
The Man with Only a Hammer
正文Body

「对一个只有锤子的人来说，一切看起来都像钉子。」芒格用这句话嘲讽单一学科的专家。经济学教授用供需解释一切，心理学家用童年创伤解释一切——他们都看不见世界的全貌。

"To a man with a hammer, everything looks like a nail." Munger mocked single-discipline experts. Economists explain everything with supply and demand; psychologists with childhood trauma — each blind to the full picture.

真正的智慧来自跨学科：复利来自数学，进化论来自生物学，激励机制来自心理学，冗余设计来自工程学。把这些模型编织成一张网，才能过滤信号与噪声。

Real wisdom comes from crossing disciplines: compound interest from math, evolution from biology, incentives from psychology, redundancy from engineering. Weave them into a lattice to filter signal from noise.

核心洞察Core Insight

单一模型让你自信，多元模型让你清醒。

"You've got to have models in your head. And you've got to array your experience on this latticework of models."

CHAPTER TWO
A Latticework of Mental Models
多元思维模型
每天醒来更聪明一点
A Little Wiser Each Morning
正文Body

芒格活了 99 岁，秘诀不是天赋，而是终身学习。他说：「我不断看到有些人并非最聪明，有时甚至不是最勤奋，但他们是学习机器——他们每晚睡觉时都比醒来时更聪明。」

Munger lived to 99 — not through genius alone, but lifelong learning. "I constantly see people rise who aren't the smartest, sometimes not even the most diligent — but they are learning machines. They go to bed a little wiser than when they woke up."

他建议掌握至少 100 个来自不同学科的基础模型，并在真实场景中交叉运用。阅读、思考、复盘——这三件事构成了他一生的日常。

He urged mastering at least 100 basic models from different fields and applying them in real situations. Reading, thinking, reviewing — these three habits defined his daily life.

核心洞察Core Insight

复利不只适用于金钱，也适用于智慧。

"Spend each day trying to be a little wiser than you were when you woke up."

Circle of Competence
能力圈
知道自己不知道什么
Knowing What You Don't Know
正文Body

芒格说：「知道自己不知道什么，比聪明更有用。」最危险的人不是无知者，而是不知道自己无知的人——他们会在能力圈之外下重注。

"Knowing what you don't know is more useful than being brilliant." The most dangerous people aren't the ignorant — they're those who don't know they're ignorant. They bet big outside their circle of competence.

他和巴菲特数十年来只在自己真正理解的领域下注。宁可错过一千个机会，也不踏入能力圈之外一步。这种克制，恰恰是他们超额收益的来源。

He and Buffett bet only in domains they truly understood for decades. They'd rather miss a thousand opportunities than step outside their circle. That restraint is the source of their excess returns.

核心洞察Core Insight

能力圈不是围墙，而是你知道自己站在哪里的地图。

"We have three buckets: yes, no, and too tough to understand. We just throw most decisions into the too-tough bucket."

CHAPTER THREE
Circle of Competence
能力圈
等待好球
Wait for the Fat Pitch
正文Body

棒球运动员泰德·威廉姆斯只打自己击球区内的球——芒格把这套逻辑用在投资上：大部分时间什么都不做，耐心等待那个「好球」出现。

Ted Williams only swung at pitches in his sweet spot — Munger applied this to investing: do nothing most of the time, patiently wait for the fat pitch.

市场每天都在诱惑你行动，但芒格说：「赚大钱的诀窍不在于买卖，而在于等待。」纪律比速度更重要，耐心本身就是一种竞争优势。

The market tempts you to act every day, but Munger said: "The big money is not in the buying or selling, but in the waiting." Discipline beats speed — patience itself is a competitive edge.

核心洞察Core Insight

不出手，也是一种决策；等待，也是一种能力。

"The big money is not in the buying or selling, but in the waiting."

Avoiding Stupidity
避免愚蠢
25 种误判心理学
The Psychology of Human Misjudgment
正文Body

芒格总结了 25 种人类认知偏误：从众心理、过度自信、沉没成本、嫉妒、确认偏误……他说，这些偏误像病毒一样潜伏在大脑里，随时可能让你做出愚蠢的决定。

Munger catalogued 25 cognitive biases: social proof, overconfidence, sunk cost, envy, confirmation bias… These lurk in the brain like viruses, ready to trigger stupid decisions at any moment.

他的对策很朴素：建立清单、避免嫉妒、远离有毒的人、不碰不懂的东西、不在愤怒时做决定。这些「反面教材」比任何正面鸡汤都更具操作性。

His countermeasures are plain: use checklists, avoid envy, stay away from toxic people, don't touch what you don't understand, never decide in anger. These "anti-lessons" beat any motivational speech.

核心洞察Core Insight

识别偏误，比积累知识更能保护你的人生。

"I've long believed that a certain system—which almost any intelligent person can learn—works way better than most people think."

Avoiding Stupidity
避免愚蠢
简单的人生原则
Simple Rules for a Good Life
正文Body

芒格的人生建议出奇地简单：和比你优秀的人在一起，读好书，保持好奇心，少抱怨，多感恩。他说：「得到你想要的东西的最好方式，是让自己配得上它。」

Munger's life advice is surprisingly simple: surround yourself with better people, read good books, stay curious, complain less, be grateful. "The best way to get what you want is to deserve what you want."

他用 99 年的生命证明：持续学习和理性思考本身就是最好的人生策略。聪明不是天赋，而是一种可以练习的习惯。

He proved it over 99 years: continuous learning and rational thinking are the best life strategy. Intelligence isn't a gift — it's a habit you can practice.

核心洞察Core Insight

配得上你想要的，比追逐你想要的更可靠。

"The best way to get what you want is to deserve what you want."
